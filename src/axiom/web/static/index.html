Research → Debate → Backtest → Risk → Review. There is deliberately no execute stage. The pipeline ends in an approval request a human acts on; it cannot place an order. Each stage separates facts — measured by the engines — from narrative, which is written by a language model. They are never merged, so you can always tell which is which.
Picking a strategy is itself a search. Every strategy in the archive is scored across walk-forward folds and the best record wins — but the best of 49 records is inflated by the act of choosing it, the same way the best of 49 coin flips looks like skill. The deflated_r figure already charges for that; raw_mean_r does not. The selection is made by arithmetic before any agent sees it, and takes a minute or two to run.

Use the terminal (axiom broker --live) if you mean to look at a funded account. Reconciliation is the part that matters: the broker's book and this platform's book drift for ordinary reasons — a manual trade, a corporate action, a fill missed during a restart — and the drift is silent. A position the broker holds that the platform does not know about is unmanaged: nothing here will size against it, stop it out, or close it.
