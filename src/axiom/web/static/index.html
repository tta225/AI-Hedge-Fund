Agent pipeline

Research → Debate → Backtest → Risk → Review. No execute stage.
How to read this page

Each stage separates facts — measured by the engines — from narrative, written by a language model. They are never merged, so you can always tell which numbers were computed and which prose was written.

There is deliberately no execute stage. The pipeline ends in an approval request a human acts on; it cannot place an order.

Picking a strategy is itself a search — what the numbers mean

Every strategy in the archive is scored across walk-forward folds and the best record wins. But the best of 49 records is inflated by the act of choosing it, the same way the best of 49 coin flips looks like skill.

deflated_r already charges for that. raw_mean_r does not. When they disagree sharply, the gap is the size of the illusion.

The selection is made by arithmetic before any agent sees it, and takes a minute or two to run.

Why reconciliation is the part that matters

The broker's book and this platform's book drift for ordinary reasons — a manual trade, a corporate action, a fill missed during a restart — and the drift is silent.

A position the broker holds that the platform does not know about is unmanaged: nothing here will size against it, stop it out, or close it.

This console never builds a venue in its real-money form — Tradovate is reached on its demo host. Use axiom broker --live in the terminal if you mean to look at a funded account.
